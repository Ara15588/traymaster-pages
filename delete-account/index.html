Delete your data

TrayMaster by DeepSleep

You can ask us to delete your TrayMaster account and the data linked to it at any time.

Delete it in the game

Open Settings, go to the Account section and press Delete next to Delete account, then confirm. The game deletes the player account that identifies you to our sign-in provider (and its link to your Google Play Games account), clears all the data it keeps on your device (progress, characters, currencies, settings and preferences), and then closes. The next time you open it, it starts as a new player. You need an internet connection; if the account cannot be deleted, nothing is removed and you can try again.

Or ask us by email

  1. Send an email to deepsleepover9000@gmail.com with the subject Delete my TrayMaster data.
  2. If you linked a Google Play Games account, tell us which one (the Google account email and your Play Games name), so we can find your account.
  3. We will confirm when your request has been processed, normally within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your player account with our sign-in provider and its link to your Google Play Games account.
  • The progress, characters, currencies, settings and preferences saved on your device.
  • By email only: any suggestions you sent from the game that we can link to you.

What we may keep

  • Records we must keep by law, such as purchase records held by Google Play.
  • Anonymous or aggregated statistics that cannot be linked back to you.
  • Data held by our advertising and analytics providers is handled under their own policies. See the privacy policy for the list.

If you only played as a guest

Guests have the same option: the in-game Delete account button removes the automatic guest player account and the progress stored on your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your device settings, also removes the progress on your device. Analytics tied only to a random identifier cannot be matched to you by us, so we cannot look it up.

Deleting your data does not cancel purchases made through Google Play. Manage those in the Google Play Store app.
