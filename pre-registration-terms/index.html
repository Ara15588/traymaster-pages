Pre-registration Reward Terms

TrayMaster by DeepSleep · Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short: pre-register for TrayMaster on Google Play, install the game with the same Google account, and you receive the exclusive Waiter tray design for free.

These terms apply to the pre-registration reward for the game TrayMaster, published by DeepSleep (“we”, “us”). By pre-registering for the reward you agree to these terms.

1. The reward

The reward is the Waiter tray, a cosmetic design for your trays in the game. It changes how a tray looks and has no effect on gameplay. It can be used on round trays, not on square trays.

2. Who can receive it

  • You must tap the pre-registration button on the TrayMaster page on Google Play while pre-registration is open in your country.
  • You must install and open the game on an Android device using the same Google account you pre-registered with.
  • The reward can be received once per Google account.

3. How you receive it

The reward is free and delivered through Google Play. When it reaches the game, a message on the main menu tells you that you received it, and you can then select the design from your tray cosmetics. Delivery needs an internet connection.

4. Limits

  • The reward has no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money or other items, and cannot be transferred to another player or account.
  • The reward is stored with your game progress. If you delete your data, or lose your progress without an account linked to it, the reward may be lost with it.
  • Where the law allows, we may correct or remove a reward obtained through fraud or abuse.

5. Availability

Pre-registration may not be available in every country. We may change or end the pre-registration campaign, as allowed by Google Play’s rules and applicable law. Your rights under the law where you live are not affected by these terms.

6. Your data

Our privacy policy explains how the game handles your information.

7. Contact

If you pre-registered and did not receive the reward, or have any question about these terms, write to DeepSleep at deepsleepover9000@gmail.com.
