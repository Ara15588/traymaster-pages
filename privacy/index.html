Privacy Policy

TrayMaster by DeepSleep · Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short: TrayMaster stores your progress on your device, can sync it to an optional account, shows ads, and uses analytics to improve the game. We do not sell your personal information, and the game is not meant for children under 13.

This policy explains what information the game TrayMaster (the “game”) collects, how DeepSleep (“we”, “us”) uses it, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By playing the game you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

The game is published by DeepSleep. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at deepsleepover9000@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Google Play Games. If you choose to link Google Play Games, we receive the player identifier that Google provides so your progress can be tied to that account.
  • Suggestions. If you send a suggestion from the game, we receive the text you write. Please do not include personal information in it.

Information collected automatically

  • Game data. Your progress, characters, settings, purchases inside the game and similar save data. It is stored on your device and, if you use an account, with our backend provider.
  • Identifiers. A randomly generated player identifier created by Unity Authentication when you start the game, an analytics user identifier, and your device’s advertising ID where your device and settings allow it.
  • Usage and gameplay events. For example which shifts you play and finish, scores, levels, energy use, shop activity, rewarded ads watched, and errors. These are sent to our analytics provider.
  • Device and technical data. Device model, operating system and version, app version, language, time zone, network type and an approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Purchase data. When you buy something, Google Play processes the payment. We receive confirmation of the purchase (the product, the time and an order identifier), not your payment card or other payment details.
  • Ad data. When ads are shown, our advertising partners receive device information such as the advertising ID and IP address, and record whether an ad was shown, clicked or completed.

3. How we use information

  • To run the game, save your progress, restore it on a new device if you use an account, and apply purchases and rewards.
  • To show rewarded and other ads, and to measure how they perform.
  • To understand how the game is played so we can balance it, fix problems and improve it.
  • To detect fraud, abuse and technical problems, and to keep the game secure.
  • To answer your messages and handle privacy requests.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performing our agreement with you (providing the game and your account), your consent (for example for analytics, and for personalised advertising where consent is required, which is collected through Google's consent form), and our legitimate interests in running, securing and improving the game.

5. Advertising

The game shows ads, including rewarded ads you can choose to watch in exchange for in-game items. Ads are provided through Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks connected to it, including Unity Ads and Google AdMob. Ads may be personalised using your advertising ID. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks for your choice about personalised ads when you first open the game, and no ads are requested until you have answered. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time in Settings (“Ad privacy options”). Elsewhere, ads may be personalised unless you opt out in your device settings.

Analytics are a separate choice. The first time you open the game we ask whether you allow analytics. Until you answer, and if you decline, the game sends no analytics. You can change your choice at any time in Settings (“Analytics”).

6. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as it is needed for the purposes above. Account and save data are kept until you ask us to delete them or the account is no longer used. Analytics and advertising data are kept by our providers according to their own retention periods. Suggestions are kept for as long as we need them to consider your feedback.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data. You can delete your player account and the data linked to it yourself in the game, under Settings → Account → Delete account, or ask us to do it by email. See how to delete your data.
  • Advertising and analytics. Change your analytics choice in the game under Settings → Analytics. Where Google’s consent form applies, change your advertising choice under Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Access, correction, portability and objection. Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have the right to access the information we hold about you, correct it, receive a copy, object to or restrict some processing, and withdraw consent you gave earlier.
  • Complaints. You may complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, write to deepsleepover9000@gmail.com. We may need to verify that a request comes from you, and we will answer within the time the law requires (generally within 30 days).

8. Children

TrayMaster is not directed to children under 13 (or under the higher age that applies in your country for consenting to data processing), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data sent between the game and our providers is encrypted in transit. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where the law requires it, they rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the game or on this page before it takes effect.
