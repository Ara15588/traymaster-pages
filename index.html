Roadmap

Available now The launch version

  • Carry and balance: load your tray, keep it steady and deliver every order without spilling.
  • All kinds of venues: cafés, fast food, bars, pizzerias, food trucks, a food court, a rooftop and more, each with harder shifts as you progress.
  • Your waiter team: recruit waiters, level them up and look after their energy between shifts.
  • Combos and tips: chain perfect deliveries to earn bigger tips.
  • Daily play: challenges, quests and daily rewards.
  • Tray designs: unlock new looks for your trays.

Free to play on Android, with optional in-game purchases. Available in 12 languages. Sign in with Google Play Games or play as a guest.

Coming soon The first content update

  • Heroes and villains food collection: a new set of dishes to collect and serve, themed on heroes and villains.
  • Weekly objective: the task system gets a bigger goal that runs all week, alongside the daily tasks.
  • Waiters’ housing: buy your waiters a place to live, from flats and RVs to houses and more. Better housing changes how fast they recover energy between shifts.
  • Nightmare challenges: much harder challenges with better rewards for players who want a real test.
